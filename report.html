✦ Speak up

Whistleblowing & Speak-Up Portal

Diraflow is committed to operating with integrity. This is your confidential space to raise concerns about unethical, illegal, or unsafe conduct — including concerns about the AI systems we are building. Your voice matters and you are protected.

🔒

Fully confidential

Your report is handled with strict confidentiality. Details are shared only with those who need to investigate.

👤

Anonymous option

You may submit anonymously. We cannot follow up, but your report will still be reviewed and acted upon.

🛡️

No retaliation

Diraflow strictly prohibits retaliation. Any manager found to have retaliated faces immediate disciplinary action.

⚖️

Legal protection

Whistleblowers are protected under Kenya's Bribery Act 2016, Leadership and Integrity Act 2012, and Public Officer Ethics Act 2003.

Policy 8.2

What to Report

The following types of concerns should be reported through this portal. If you are unsure whether something qualifies — report it anyway. We would rather investigate something that turns out to be minor than miss something serious.

📋

Policy violations

Violations of any policy in the Diraflow Company Policy Handbook — including AI ethics, data governance, research integrity, and employment policies.

⚠️

Unsafe AI development

Unsafe or harmful AI development practices — including building prohibited AI systems, bypassing ethics review, or ignoring safety testing requirements.

💰

Fraud & financial misconduct

Fraud, bribery, corruption, embezzlement, expense manipulation, or any financial irregularity involving company or client funds.

🗄️

Data breaches & privacy

Data breaches, unauthorised access to personal data, privacy violations, or misuse of client or contributor data in breach of our data governance policy.

🤝

Discrimination & harassment

Discrimination, bullying, harassment, or intimidation based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other characteristic.

🔇

Suppression of safety concerns

Pressure from any person to suppress, conceal, ignore, or not report safety concerns, ethical issues, or potential misconduct.

🧾

Intellectual property abuse

Unauthorised use, sharing, or theft of intellectual property — including client datasets, model weights, or proprietary Diraflow methodologies.

🌍

Environmental violations

Deliberate circumvention of Diraflow's environmental sustainability commitments — including wasteful compute use or misrepresentation of energy usage.

❓

Other concerns

Any other conduct you believe is unethical, illegal, or contrary to Diraflow's values — even if it doesn't fit neatly into the categories above.

Policy 8.4

Your protections

Zero tolerance for retaliation Diraflow prohibits retaliation against any person who raises a concern in good faith. Retaliation includes demotion, dismissal, pay reduction, exclusion, hostile treatment, and harassment. Any manager found to have retaliated against a whistleblower will face immediate disciplinary action — up to and including termination.

What "good faith" means

You are protected if you genuinely believe the concern you're reporting is real — even if an investigation later finds no wrongdoing. You are not protected if you knowingly make a false report to harm someone.

Legal protection in Kenya

You may additionally be protected under the Bribery Act 2016 (Kenya), the Leadership and Integrity Act 2012 (Kenya), and the Public Officer Ethics Act 2003 (Kenya).

Investigation process All reports are reviewed by senior leadership within the timeframes stated above. Investigations are conducted impartially and confidentially. You will be updated on the outcome where you have provided contact details and consented to follow-up. Diraflow will not disclose who made a report without your consent, except where required by law.
