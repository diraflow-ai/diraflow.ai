Data Organization & Categorization

Data Classification

Transform raw and unstructured data into meaningful categories that power accurate AI and machine learning systems with expert classification services.

← Back to Solutions
Data Classification is a critical data annotation process that organizes raw and unstructured data into meaningful categories to improve the accuracy and performance of AI and machine learning systems. Our Data Classification services help businesses transform text, images, audio, and video into structured datasets that enable faster training, better predictions, and smarter automation.

We provide scalable and precise classification solutions tailored to your business needs. Using customized taxonomies, domain-trained annotators, and multi-level quality assurance, we ensure consistent, bias-controlled, and high-accuracy labeled data for a wide range of industries including healthcare, finance, e-commerce, automotive, media, and customer support.

Our team supports single-label, multi-label, and hierarchical classification workflows to handle complex datasets. Whether you need document categorization, product tagging, content moderation, sentiment-based text classification, or scene-based image classification, our human-in-the-loop approach ensures reliable and production-ready training data.

📝 Text Classification

Organize text data into predefined categories such as intent, topic, sentiment, and document type. Our annotators categorize documents, emails, customer feedback, and support tickets with precision, enabling faster information retrieval and automated routing in customer service platforms, content management systems, and NLP pipelines.

Common use cases:

  • Email classification for spam detection and prioritization
  • Customer support ticket routing and categorization
  • News article topic classification and aggregation
  • Intent detection for chatbots and virtual assistants
  • Document type classification in enterprise systems
  • Product feedback categorization for insights and analytics

😊 Sentiment & Emotion Classification

Analyze and classify the emotional tone and sentiment expressed in text, audio, and video content. Our experts identify positive, negative, and neutral sentiments along with nuanced emotions like joy, anger, frustration, and trust. This service enables brand monitoring, customer satisfaction analysis, and content recommendation systems across multiple modalities.

Common use cases:

  • Social media sentiment analysis and brand monitoring
  • Customer satisfaction and NPS score analysis
  • Review classification for e-commerce platforms
  • Content recommendation based on emotional context
  • Call center quality assurance and customer interaction analysis
  • Market research and consumer behavior insights

🖼️ Image Classification

Categorize images into object categories, product types, and scene classifications. Our annotators classify images for e-commerce product catalogs, medical imaging systems, autonomous vehicles, and content moderation platforms. We handle diverse image types including photographs, diagrams, screenshots, and medical scans with domain expertise.

Common use cases:

  • E-commerce product categorization and attribute tagging
  • Medical image classification (X-ray, CT, MRI)
  • Scene and environment classification for autonomous vehicles
  • Wildlife and species classification for conservation
  • Content moderation and inappropriate image filtering
  • Retail shelf image classification for inventory management

🎵 Audio Classification

Classify audio recordings by language, speaker type, sound event detection, and acoustic characteristics. Our audio specialists categorize music, ambient sounds, speech patterns, and environmental audio for speech recognition systems, music streaming platforms, and acoustic analysis applications. We support multilingual and accented speech classification.

Common use cases:

  • Language and dialect identification in multilingual systems
  • Speaker type classification (male, female, child, elderly)
  • Audio event detection (gunshot, siren, breaking glass)
  • Music genre and mood classification for streaming platforms
  • Environmental sound classification for smart home systems
  • Audio quality and background noise classification

🎬 Video Classification

Categorize video content by activity recognition, event tagging, and context labeling. Our video specialists classify actions, interactions, scenes, and events occurring throughout video sequences. This service enables intelligent video search, content recommendation, surveillance analytics, and sports analysis systems.

Common use cases:

  • Activity recognition for fitness and sports analytics
  • Event detection in surveillance and security footage
  • Video content classification for streaming platforms
  • Anomaly detection in surveillance systems
  • Workplace safety and compliance monitoring
  • Video search and retrieval optimization

🌳 Hierarchical Classification

Organize data using parent-child category relationships and taxonomy structures. Our experts create and apply multi-level classification hierarchies that enable more nuanced understanding of data. This is particularly valuable for e-commerce product catalogs, medical coding systems, and industry-specific taxonomies where categories have natural hierarchies.

Common use cases:

  • E-commerce product taxonomy (category → subcategory → type)
  • Medical coding with ICD-10 hierarchies
  • Industry classification systems
  • News and content organization with topic hierarchies
  • Ontology creation for domain-specific applications
  • Organizational knowledge structure development

🛡️ Content Moderation & Safety Classification

Classify content for safety, appropriateness, and compliance requirements across text, images, audio, and video. Our safety experts identify inappropriate content, harmful material, regulatory violations, and policy violations with consistency and accuracy. This service protects users, maintains platform integrity, and ensures compliance with content policies and legal requirements.

Common use cases:

  • Inappropriate content filtering for social media platforms
  • Hate speech and harassment detection
  • Child safety and exploitation prevention
  • Regulatory compliance and legal content review
  • Misinformation and fake news identification
  • Violent, explicit, and sensitive content flagging
