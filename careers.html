Image Annotation Specialist

Remote · Contract · AI Training Data

Join our AI training data team and help improve machine learning models by accurately labeling and reviewing image datasets.

Responsibilities

  • Annotate images using provided guidelines
  • Identify objects, categories, attributes, and visual elements
  • Maintain high accuracy and consistency across datasets
  • Review annotations and correct mistakes
  • Follow detailed project instructions
  • Provide feedback when guidelines are unclear

Requirements

  • Excellent attention to detail
  • Ability to follow written instructions
  • Strong visual recognition skills
  • Reliable internet connection
  • Basic computer skills
  • Previous annotation experience is an advantage

Image Quality Assessment Specialist

Remote · Contract · AI Evaluation

Evaluate images and AI-generated visual content to ensure accuracy, realism, and quality standards.

Responsibilities

  • Compare images and determine quality differences
  • Evaluate realism, clarity, composition, and visual accuracy
  • Identify defects and inconsistencies
  • Provide structured quality feedback
  • Review AI-generated images against evaluation criteria

Requirements

  • Strong visual judgment
  • High attention to small details
  • Ability to make objective decisions
  • Understanding of image quality concepts
  • Experience with digital images or AI evaluation is a plus
